Montréal Construction Observatory

About this map

This interactive map helps visualize the concentration of construction worksites across the Island of Montréal. It's designed for anyone who wants to explore where construction activity is happening. You are able to compare between boroughs and see how construction affect each one differently

How to use this map

  1. Borough level: Click on a borough to see its construction concentration.
  2. Hexagon level: Zoom in to see construction density as hexagons.

Data sources

All from the Ville de Montréal open data portal, under the CC BY 4.0 licence:

Ville de Montréal Open Data – Entraves et travaux en cours

Government of Canada Open Data – Montreal Boroughs

Limitations

This version shows where construction is concentrated, but it can't measure how severely it affects residents or businesses.
